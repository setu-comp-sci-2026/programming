# 9. Apply what you learned to Assignment 2
The Question, Rule and Troubleshooter classes are domain-independent for this yes/no rule format. You may reuse them unchanged if they suit your assignment and you can explain them. Do not simply rename the PC example and submit it.

## Transfer exercise
Choose your own expert-system topic. On paper or in a separate practice JSON file, write three questions with unique keys and at least three rules, including one with multiple conditions. Check that each rule key exists among your questions. Predict the advice for four answer sets before testing. Think about rule order and what happens if no rule matches.

## Explain your design
- What does each class manage? Which methods could you reuse without changes?
- Where does user input belong? Where does rule matching belong?
- What must be changed for your topic? What does your assignment brief require beyond this lab?
- Why are knowledge and consultation history separate files?



Check your assignment brief for all requirements. The lab does not implement every possible assignment feature.  The lab classes are not written using properties, this is something that you can add to improve the classes.
