# Mini project - Cinema ticket system

You are going to build a small cinema ticket program.

Create **07_cinema.py**.

## Step 1 - Ask for age

Ask the customer for their age.

## Step 2 - Choose a basic ticket price

Use these prices:

| Customer | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| Under 16 | €7.00 |
| 16 to 64 | €12.00 |
| 65+ | €8.00 |

Store the price in a variable called:

~~~python
ticket_price
~~~

## Step 3 - Display the price

Example:

~~~
Ticket price: €12.0
~~~

## Step 4 - Add a student discount

Customers aged 16 to 64 can receive a student discount.

Ask:

~~~
Are you a student? yes/no:
~~~

If they answer `yes`, reduce the normal €12 ticket to €10.

Do not ask children or customers aged 65+ this question.

## Step 5 - Add a film age restriction

At the start of the program, ask:

~~~
Film age rating:
~~~

For example, the user might enter `12`, `15`, or `18`.

The customer can only buy a ticket if:

~~~python
age >= film_rating
~~~

If they are too young, display:

~~~
Sorry, you are too young for this film
~~~

and do not calculate a ticket price.

## Step 6 - Test carefully

Create a test table before running your program.

| age | rating | student | expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | 12 | - | Too young |
| 12 | 12 | - | Child price |
| 15 | 15 | - | Child price |
| 16 | 15 | yes | Student price |
| 30 | 18 | no | Standard price |
| 65 | 15 | - | 65+ price |

Add at least three tests of your own.

## Challenge

Add a day of the week.

On Tuesday, every customer who is old enough to see the film pays €6.

Think carefully about where this condition should appear.
