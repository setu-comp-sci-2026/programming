# Your first `if` statement - Solutions

## Cinema age check

~~~python
age = int(input("Enter your age: "))

if age >= 18:
    print("You can watch the film")

print("Enjoy your visit to the cinema")
~~~

For `17`:

~~~
Enjoy your visit to the cinema
~~~

For `18` or `25`:

~~~
You can watch the film
Enjoy your visit to the cinema
~~~

## Free delivery

~~~python
order_total = float(input("Order total: €"))

if order_total >= 50:
    print("You qualify for free delivery")

print("Thank you for your order")
~~~

The important boundary value is:

~~~
50
~~~

At exactly €50, the customer should receive free delivery.
