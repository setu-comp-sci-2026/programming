# Assignment Discussion

After submission, you may be asked to take part in a short interview  about your submitted work.

You may be asked to:

- explain a section of your code
- explain why you used an `if`, `elif` or `else`
- explain a Boolean expression
- explain the purpose of one of your functions
- identify the value returned by a function
- explain a test case
- describe what happens for a particular input

You may also be asked to make one small modification to your program.

For example:

> Change the minimum age from 18 to 21.

or:

> Change the program so that a customer with exactly €50 qualifies for the discount.

or:

> Add one additional recommendation.

or:

> Change a function so that it accepts a value as a parameter.

The purpose of this discussion is to demonstrate your understanding of the program you submitted. The mark given for the interview will be based on your demonstration of your understanding of the code and design of the program. The mark will be used as a multiplier for the mark for the assignment., e.g. 

Mark for assignmnent (before interview)  = 70%

Mark for interview = 50% 

Overall mark for assignment and interview = 70% * 50% = 35%
