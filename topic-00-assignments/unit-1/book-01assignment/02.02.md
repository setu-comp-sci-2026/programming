# The Challenge

Your task is to create a Python application that helps a user make a decision.

You may choose a theme that interests you.

Possible ideas include:

- What film should I watch?
- What game should I play?
- What should I eat?
- What workout should I do?
- What should I listen to?
- What should I wear today?
- Where should I go for the weekend?
- Which pet would suit me?
- What should I study tonight?
- Which coffee should I order?
- What activity should I do today?
- What gift should I buy?

You may also propose your own idea.

Your application should be more than a simple calculator. It should use several pieces of information supplied by the user to make a meaningful decision or recommendation.
