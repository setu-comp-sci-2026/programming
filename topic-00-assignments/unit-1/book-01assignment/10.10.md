# Testing

You must test your application carefully.

For example:

| Test Num | Age   |Budget    |Weather       | Hours  |Expected Result               |Actual Result
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -- | -- | ----- | - | ------ | ------ |
| 1                                                     | 20 | 50 | sunny | 5 | Hiking | Hiking |
| 2                                                     | 17 | 10 | rainy | 2 | Gaming | Gaming |
| 3                                                     | 25 | 15 | rainy | 3 | Movie  | Movie  |

Your tests should include:

- normal values
- different possible program outcomes
- at least two boundary-value tests

For example, if your program contains:

```python
if age >= 18:

```

you might test:

```text
17
18
19

```
