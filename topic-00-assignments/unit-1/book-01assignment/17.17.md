# Marking Guidance

## Program functionality – 25 marks

Marks will be awarded for:

- a working interactive application
- appropriate user input
- meaningful results
- several possible outcomes
- implementation of the personalised requirement

---

## Selection and decision-making – 25 marks

Marks will be awarded for:

- correct use of `if`
- correct use of `if-else`
- appropriate use of `if-elif-else`
- compound Boolean expressions
- suitable use of `and`, `or` or `not`
- correct handling of boundary values
- meaningful decision-making

---

## Functions – 20 marks

Marks will be awarded for:

- appropriate decomposition into functions
- meaningful function names
- appropriate parameters
- use of return values
- avoiding unnecessary repetition

---

## Data types and input – 10 marks

Marks will be awarded for:

- suitable variables
- suitable data types
- appropriate use of `input()`
- appropriate type conversion

---

## Code quality – 10 marks

Marks will be awarded for:

- readability
- indentation
- meaningful naming
- sensible organisation
- appropriate comments

## Reflection and Design Documents – 10 marks

Marks will be awarded for:

- Clarity
- Conciseness
- Quality of design
- Evidence of planning
- Thoughtfulness of reflection
- Evidence of learning
- Identification of challenges
- Testing considerations
- Appropriate use/declaration of resources and AI
- Overall completeness

---

You may be expected to attend an interview
