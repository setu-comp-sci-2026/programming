# 7. Selection

Your program must demonstrate appropriate use of selection.

You must include:

- at least one simple `if` statement
- at least one `if-else` statement
- at least one `if-elif-else` statement
- at least one compound Boolean expression

For example:

```python
if age >= 18 and budget >= 20:
    ...

```

You should use the following where appropriate:

```python
and
or
not

```

Your selection statements should contribute meaningfully to the behaviour of the program.

Adding unnecessary `if` statements simply to meet the requirements will not gain additional marks.
