You must carry out your own self assessment on your work and show this by including this in your ‘reflection.txt’ file (at the root of your project) Use the detailed  rubric to reflect on the work you’ve done, and critically assess yourself accordingly. You must assign marks for each section.

Allow plenty of time to complete it properly.

We suggest that you finish all coding before completing the reflection. You should have added your code comments already.

Detail for each row of the rubric, and any notes on specific issues that arose for that element

The [template sample](./archives/reflection.pdf) is a sample of how you might fill in the reflection.

You must mark yourself. You must supply evidence for those marks.
