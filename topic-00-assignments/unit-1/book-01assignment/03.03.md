# Example of susch a system

Imagine you decide to create:

## What Should I Do This Weekend?

Your program might ask:

```text
What is your name? Sarah
What is your age? 20
What is your budget? €35
What is the weather like? sunny
How many hours do you have available? 4
Do you want to be indoors or outdoors? outdoors

```

Your program could then use these answers to produce:

```text
Hi Sarah!

Based on your answers, we recommend going for a hike.

You have enough time, the weather is suitable and this activity is within your budget.

Enjoy your weekend!

```

Different combinations of answers should produce different recommendations.
