# 34. The Bigger AI Picture

Expert systems are an important part of the history of Artificial Intelligence.

They belong to an approach generally known as **symbolic AI**, where knowledge can be explicitly represented using facts, symbols and rules.

The developer and domain expert decide what knowledge the system needs and how that knowledge should be represented.

Modern machine-learning and generative-AI systems operate very differently. Rather than requiring programmers to explicitly write every rule, they can learn patterns from large quantities of data.

Your expert system will not be ChatGPT.

It is intentionally much smaller and more understandable.

In fact, one advantage of your system is that you should be able to answer an important question:

> **Why did the computer make that decision?**

You should be able to follow the user's answers through your Knowledge Base, identify the rules that were applied and explain how the system reached its conclusion.

By the end of this project, you will have progressed from writing individual `if` statements to designing a complete program capable of **representing knowledge, applying rules, making decisions and explaining its reasoning**.
