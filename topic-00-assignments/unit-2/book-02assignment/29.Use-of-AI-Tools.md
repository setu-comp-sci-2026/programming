# 29. Use of AI Tools

AI tools can be useful when learning and developing software.

You may use AI to support activities such as:

- understanding a programming concept
- interpreting an error message
- discussing possible designs
- debugging code
- suggesting test cases

However, the submitted project must represent your understanding and development work.

You are responsible for all code and design decisions contained in your submission.

You must be able to:

- explain your Knowledge Base
- explain your rules
- explain your inference engine
- explain your classes
- explain how data moves through your program
- modify your own solution

Your development checkpoints and final demonstration form part of the evidence used to assess your understanding.
