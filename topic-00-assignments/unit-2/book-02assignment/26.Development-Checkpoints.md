# 26. Development Checkpoints

During the semester, you will be asked to demonstrate your progress.

These are not intended to catch you out.

They provide an opportunity to demonstrate how your system has developed and to receive feedback.

You may be asked to:

> Show me your current Knowledge Base.

> Show me how a rule is represented.

> Add a question.

> Show me how the program finds a recommendation.

> Show me how your JSON is loaded.

> Explain why you created this class.

> Show me an example of inheritance.

> Change one piece of expert knowledge.

Regular development is an important part of the project.
