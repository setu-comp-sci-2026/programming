# 32. What Makes a Strong Knowledge Base?

A strong project does not simply have a large Knowledge Base.

We will consider whether:

- questions are relevant
- rules make sense
- recommendations are meaningful
- rules connect appropriately to recommendations
- knowledge is represented consistently
- the Knowledge Base can be maintained
- new knowledge can be introduced without redesigning the entire program
- the inference engine can process the knowledge generally

Twenty thoughtful rules are considerably better than one hundred almost identical rules.
