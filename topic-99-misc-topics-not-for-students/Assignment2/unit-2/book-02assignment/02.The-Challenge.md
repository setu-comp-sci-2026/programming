# 2. The Challenge

Choose an area in which your program can act as a small expert.

Possible examples include:


- Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Expert
- Cybersecurity Adviser
- Plant Care Expert
- Garden Plant Adviser
- Pet Suitability Adviser
- Film Recommendation Expert
- Gaming Recommendation Expert
- Study Strategy Adviser
- Travel Recommendation Expert
- Sports Training Adviser
- Car Troubleshooting Expert
- Laptop Recommendation Expert

You can't use PC Troubleshooting Expert as we are using this example in class

You may propose your own topic.

Your chosen topic must provide enough scope for a meaningful collection of questions, rules and recommendations.

Your topic must be approved before significant development begins.
