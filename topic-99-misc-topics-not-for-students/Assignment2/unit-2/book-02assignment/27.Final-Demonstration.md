# 27. Final Demonstration

As part of the assessment, you will demonstrate your completed Expert System. As part of this demonstration, you should be able to explain how any part of the system works (via code)

You should be prepared to demonstrate:

### Consultation

Run your expert system and reach a recommendation.

### Reasoning

Explain why the recommendation was produced.

### Knowledge Base

Show how questions, rules and recommendations are represented.

### CRUD

Add, view, edit and delete knowledge.

### Persistence

Make a change, save it, restart the program and demonstrate that the change remains.

### Object-Oriented Design

Explain important classes and objects.

### Inheritance and Polymorphism

Demonstrate where these are used.
