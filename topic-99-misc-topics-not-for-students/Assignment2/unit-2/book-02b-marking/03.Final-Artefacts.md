# 03. Final Artefacts

You are asked to submit a zipped file containing the following:
1. Your complete Python project
2. Your Knowledge Base JSON file(s)
3. Your original Knowledge Base design
4. Your testing evidence
5. A short project reflection
6. A video recording (7 minutes) 

## 1, 2. Complete Python Project

The code for your expert system should be included in the the code for your expert system. This should be in a subdirectory.
This should include the following:

- all Python .py files
- all JSON files
- a README file explaining how to run the program

## 3. Your original Knowledge Base design
Write a document explaining how you designed your knowledge base. 

## 4. Testing Evidence

Show how you tested your expert system.

## 5. Reflection Document

Your reflection should discuss:

- how your system changed during development
- how you represented knowledge
- how your design changed when you learned classes and objects
- the most difficult aspect of the project
- something you would improve with more time
## 6. Video recording

This video should be around 7 minutes long and show
- the system running
- the testing evidence
- the structure of your knowledge base
- the overall structure of your system




## Discretionary Marks
There are 10 discretionary marks available. This means that to achieve a full mark you must have completed all the requirements and shown evidence of completion of extra requirements worth 10%. 


## Interview
You may be asked to present your work in person at the interview. This will involve explaining your submission, how it works, and a question and answer session where we will ask you questions about any part of your submissions. 

The date of the interview will be published closer to the time. The mark of the interview will be used as a multiplier for the final mark of the assignment. 

If you are invited for an interview:
 - attendance is mandatory and failure to attend will leave to 0% mark (which as multiplier will lead to an overall final mark of 0%).
    
(else) If you are not invited for an interview:
- the mark for the interview will be 100% (no multiplier).  

During the interview, you may be asked (for instance) to demonstrate some of the following:

Run your expert system and reach a recommendation.

### Reasoning

Explain why the recommendation was produced.

### Knowledge Base

Show how questions, rules and recommendations are represented.

### CRUD

Add, view, edit and delete knowledge.

### Persistence

Make a change, save it, restart the program and demonstrate that the change remains.

### Object-Oriented Design

Explain important classes and objects.
