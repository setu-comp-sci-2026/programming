# 24. Minimum Requirements

Your finished project should normally contain:

- approximately **15–20+ meaningful questions/data points**
- at least **20 meaningful rules**
- approximately **8–10 possible recommendations/conclusions**
- more than one type of question
- meaningful use of lists
- meaningful use of dictionaries
- CRUD functionality
- persistent JSON storage
- an inference/decision engine
- explanation of recommendations
- appropriate exception handling
- appropriate classes and objects
- an inheritance hierarchy
- an example of polymorphism
- evidence of testing

These figures are intended to indicate the expected **scale** of the project.

Simply creating large numbers of repetitive questions or rules will not result in a higher mark.
