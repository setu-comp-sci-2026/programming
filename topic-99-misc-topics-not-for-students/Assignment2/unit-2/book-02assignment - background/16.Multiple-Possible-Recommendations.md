# 16. Multiple Possible Recommendations

Real decisions are not always based on one perfect match.

Your system may find that several recommendations match some of the user's answers.

Where appropriate, you may design a simple **rule-match score**.

For example:

```text
Possible Power Supply Problem
Matched 4 of 5 relevant conditions

Possible Battery Problem
Matched 2 of 4 relevant conditions
```

Your program could then rank the possible recommendations.

If you display a percentage, be careful with your terminology.

For example:

```text
Rule Match: 80%
```

does **not** necessarily mean:

```text
There is an 80% probability that the power supply is faulty.
```

It simply describes how strongly the user's answers matched the rules that you designed.
