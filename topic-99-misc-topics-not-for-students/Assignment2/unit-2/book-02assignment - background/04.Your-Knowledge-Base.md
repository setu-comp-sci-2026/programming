# 4. Your Knowledge Base

The **Knowledge Base is a central part of this assignment**.

It should contain at least three types of information:

### Questions

Information that the system needs from the user.

### Rules

Knowledge describing how different answers relate to conclusions.

### Recommendations / Conclusions

The possible results that the expert system can produce.

Conceptually:

```text
KNOWLEDGE BASE
│
├── Questions
│
├── Rules
│
└── Recommendations
```
